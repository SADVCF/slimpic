Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your images stay on your device

SlimPic compresses images entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No image data is ever uploaded to our servers — because we don't have any servers involved in the compression process.

What data do we collect?

We collect minimal anonymous analytics to understand how the tool is used (page views, general usage patterns). We do not collect:

  • Your images or image contents
  • Your name or email address
  • Any personally identifiable information

Third-party libraries

We load two open-source libraries from jsDelivr CDN: browser-image-compression and JSZip. These are used solely for compression and ZIP creation in your browser.
