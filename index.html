Frequently asked questions

Is this image compressor really free?
Yes. SlimPic is completely free to use with no limits, no ads, and no registration required.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. All compression happens locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device.
What image formats are supported?
JPEG, PNG, and WebP. Support for GIF and AVIF is coming soon.
How much can I reduce image file size?
Typically 50–90% depending on the image and quality setting. Most JPEG photos compress by 70%+ with barely visible quality loss.
What quality setting should I use?
75% is the sweet spot for most photos. For web use, 60–70% is usually fine. For high-quality prints, use 85%+.
